# Goal profiles — Coach's defaults per goal (DEC-147)

The balanced base (COACH.md) applies to every goal; a profile sets the numbers on top. A client may have a main goal and a
secondary one. Numbers marked `[to source]` are general knowledge until `EVIDENCE.md` confirms or corrects them — say so in
a report that uses them. Planner owns this file.

## Balance numbers by level

| Level | Min working sets / area / week | Largest pairing ratio | Sessions |
|---|---|---|---|
| Beginner (< 6 months regular) | ~3–4 `[to source]` | ~1.5 : 1 | 2–3 full-body |
| Intermediate | ~6 `[to source]` | ~1.5 : 1 | 3–5 |
| Rehab / returning | client's clinician first; the weak side may get extra work | — | as tolerated |

Pull may exceed push (shoulder health) `[to source]`.

## Progression (all goals)

The coach's advice rule (Planner's call 2026-10-26; the app's own rules wait for req-149, Emilio):
- **Up one step** when every work set hit its target and effort was Easy, or Medium with the target beaten by 2+ reps.
  One step = that exercise's `weightStep` (machines 5, Leg Press 10, dumbbells 2, kettlebells next bell, barbell next plate
  pair). A step larger than ~10% of the load → add reps first `[to source]`.
- **Hold** on Hard or missed reps. **Change something** (reps, sets, variation) after 3+ stalled sessions (calibration
  sessions don't count).
- **A lighter week** when effort climbs and reps fall across sessions, or every ~4–8 weeks for intermediates `[to source]`.
- Effort is Easy · Medium · Hard per exercise (the app's scale); there is no per-set effort or RIR field (→ For Planner).

## Profiles

| Goal | Reps / load | Rest | Conditioning | Outside the gym |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Strength** | main lifts 3–6 heavy; accessories 8–12 `[to source]` | 2–3 min on main lifts | light, kept apart from heavy days | sleep, protein |
| **Muscle** | 6–15, near failure; ~10+ sets / muscle / week `[to source]` | 1–2 min, 2–3 on big lifts | light | protein, slight surplus |
| **Lean / fat loss** | keep loads heavy to keep muscle; volume may drop a little | as strength / muscle | added, not instead of strength | the deficit does the fat loss: protein, steps, sleep; aim ~0.5–1 % bodyweight / week `[to source]` |
| **General health** | 8–15, 2+ strength days | 1–2 min | the public-health activity minutes `[to source]` | daily movement, sleep |
| **Endurance / running** | 2 strength days, 6–12, legs + core | 1–2 min | the main work; strength supports it | sleep, fuelling |
| **Older adults** | strength + power (fast lifting) + balance `[to source]` | as needed | walking, balance work | protein, daily steps |

**Time vs results:** a session shorter than the profile needs (short rests on heavy lifts) trades results for time — say
so in the plan, don't hide it.
