**Choices the spec left open**

- Wording, today: `You left this workout open — saved as finished at 10:50, your last set.`
- Not today: `… saved as finished at 19:00 on Mon, Oct 12, your last set.` — `weekdayDate` (History's shared "weekday + short
  date", locale `toLocaleDateString`, year added only if not this year). "Today" = local `dateKey` of finish vs now.
- HH:MM is local, 24-hour, zero-padded (`getHours`/`getMinutes`), not locale-formatted.
- The auto-complete summary shows no left-open line (Scope 4 asks only for the same time). Rare there: it mounts right after
  the last set.
- The Finish screen computes the time at mount, the store at Save. If someone sits on Finish across the 3 h mark the screen's
  minutes and the saved time can differ; not worth an API change (store `finishWorkout` args unchanged).
- A **Skip set** tap writes `loggedAt` (req-212), so it counts as a "last set"; Finish / Skip-exercise skips have none and
  never count (AC4).
- `lastSetAt` ignores an unreadable `loggedAt` (Date.parse NaN).
