- Breathing: one step uses "Exhale" (Ab Crunch Machine had it in a cue at first; that cue was replaced).
  Plank's step says "breathing steadily". There are no breathing words outside steps.

  Mountain Climber and Battle Ropes say "until the timer ends".
- Near-duplicate pairs: none are above 0.85; the closest is 0.71 (see below).

  position or grip ("on your back", "close-grip"). **No tag errors found for req-136.**

#### Back Squat — `Barbell_Squat`

squat · fam-squat · weight-reps · barbell · **quads**, **glute-max**, adductors, hamstrings, lower-back

> A squat with a barbell across the upper back that trains the quads and glutes.

**Form cues:** Brace before you descend · Knees track over the toes · Whole foot stays on the floor

**Steps:**

1. Set the bar in a rack at about shoulder height and step under it so it rests across your upper back.
2. Grip the bar, stand up to unrack it and step back with your feet about shoulder width apart.
3. Brace your core and sit down between your heels, pushing your knees out over your toes.
4. Go as deep as you can with a flat back, aiming for thighs at least parallel to the floor.
5. Drive up through your whole foot until you are standing tall.

**Mistakes:** Knees cave inward on the way up · Heels lift off the floor · Hips rise before the chest

#### Deadlift — `Barbell_Deadlift`

hinge · fam-deadlift · weight-reps · barbell · **glute-max**, **hamstrings**, **lower-back**, quads, traps, forearms

> A barbell lifted from the floor to standing that trains the glutes, hamstrings and lower back.

**Form cues:** Bar over the mid foot · Flat back, chest up · Push the floor away

**Steps:**

1. Stand with your feet about hip width apart and the bar over your mid foot.
2. Hinge down and grip the bar just outside your legs.
3. Bend your knees until your shins touch the bar, then flatten your back and brace.
4. Stand up by pushing through the floor, keeping the bar close to your legs.
5. Finish standing tall with your hips and knees straight.
6. Lower the bar by pushing your hips back, then bending your knees once it passes them.

**Mistakes:** Rounding the lower back off the floor · Hips shoot up before the bar moves · Bar drifts away from the legs
