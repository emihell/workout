**What changed**
- `exerciseLibrary.js`:
  - `staple` and `difficulty` are added to `OWN_FIELDS` and `NEW_FIELDS`, so `staple ⇒ common` falls out of the
    existing non-common check.
  - Both are emitted in `withOwnFields`, and both are validated: staple must be a boolean, and difficulty must be
    in `DIFFICULTY`.
  - `COMMON_COUNT_RANGE` goes from 170–200 to 200–240.
- `exerciseCatalog.js`: `searchCommonFirst` splits on `item.staple`. The `common` key is kept for its single caller
  (`views/Exercises.jsx`).
- `common.js`:
  - 40 rows, 15 display names, and the 13 aliases on existing rows.
  - `NOT_STAPLES` (26), and `DIFFICULTY_LISTS`: every common entry sits in exactly one list.
  - A `GROUP_EXCEPTIONS` entry for `Flutter_Kicks` (free-db tags it glutes).
  - The Set and the Map are built inside `commonExercises()`. At first they were built at module level, which
    pushed the main chunk to 345.31 kB, because a top-level `new Set` is a side effect the bundler keeps. After the
    move it is back to 340.97 kB (L-026).
- `own-exercises.js`: 13 entries with legacy fields. `level` equals `difficulty`; `instructions` are our steps.
- `text.js`: text for the 40, under the req-138 validators.
- Scripts:
  - `scripts/library-gap.mjs` is new.
  - `scripts/originality.mjs` gets `--runs-only`.
  - Both default to `.vendor-cache/`, which is added to `.gitignore` (planning's call).
- `src/req-140.test.js`: 10 tests.
  - Batch 1 as agreed; no own add shares a name key with a free-db entry; `level === difficulty`.
  - The 17 aliases as first shown hits.
  - Staples: all prior common entries are staples, `staple ⇒ common` is shown with a failing fixture, a non-staple
    appears only behind Show more, and a query with no staple hit shows rest directly.
  - Difficulty: in the list, off-list fixture, and the anchors.
