## The scanning agents' brief (verbatim)

# Brief: fresh-eyes review of a workout app's exercise library

You are reviewing the exercise library of a browser workout-logging app, as an experienced coach who is also a careful
editor. Read ONLY the files in this folder:
- `BRIEF.md` (this file);
- `fixed-lists.json`;
- `all-shown-names.txt` (every entry's id, shown name and aliases, for spotting clashes and inconsistencies);
- your assigned `entries-N.json`.

Don't open anything else.

## Who the app is for

**Beginner-first, with room for advanced.** The main user is a first-time gym-goer building a simple routine; advanced
lifters must still find their lifts. Names, text and difficulty should make sense to a beginner and not annoy an
experienced lifter.

## What to review, for every entry in your file

1. **Name** (`shownName`): is it the name most gym-goers would use and type? `originalName` is the entry's underlying
   source name and can't change. `shownName` is our display name when `hasDisplayName` is true. To rename, give a new
   display name. It must differ from `originalName` exactly, unless you want to *remove* the display name, which is
   allowed only when `originalName` is already the clean name.
2. **Aliases:** add names people actually type for this exact exercise (common alternative names, abbreviations like
   "DB"/"BB"/"RDL", plural or singular only when it matters). Remove anything wrong, misleading, or ambiguous (an alias
   that also fits a different exercise). No bulk lists of long phrasings.
3. **Text** (`description`, `formCues`, `steps`, `mistakes`): natural, clear, correct, the way a good coach talks.
   Some entries read stilted or over-engineered, as if written to avoid common phrasing. **Restore natural wording**
   wherever you see that: plain, standard gym English is good. Pay particular attention to these ids (they are known to
   read stilted): Rope_Jumping, Hanging_Leg_Raise, Reverse_Crunch, Barbell_Lunge, Stiff-Legged_Dumbbell_Deadlift,
   Pallof_Press, Reverse_Barbell_Curl, Sit-Up, Thigh_Adductor, Knee_Hip_Raise_On_Parallel_Bars, Dumbbell_Lunges,
   Preacher_Curl, Leverage_Chest_Press, Russian_Twist, Close-Grip_Barbell_Bench_Press, Plank, Leg_Extensions,
   Side_Bridge, own-single-leg-rdl, EZ-Bar_Skullcrusher, Glute_Kickback, Single_Leg_Glute_Bridge, own-bird-dog.
   Review every entry anyway. Fix anything technically wrong or unsafe.
4. **Tags:** check they are correct:
   - `primary`/`secondary` muscles: ids from `muscleTree`, tagged at the finest node that is true;
   - `pattern`, `equipmentList`, `logAs`, `unilateral`;
   - `difficulty`: skill, coordination and setup, not strength. Anchors: beginner = Leg Press, Lat Pulldown, Plank,
     Seated Leg Curl; intermediate = Back Squat, Deadlift, Walking Lunge, Pull-Up; advanced = Power Clean, Pistol Squat;
   - `staple`: a standard commercial-gym exercise that should rank first in search. Change it only with a strong
     reason;
   - `family`: variants of one movement share a family.

**Only change what is actually better.** Leaving an entry as it is is a fine outcome. Don't rewrite for the sake of it.

## Hard rules (a change that breaks one is rejected)

- **Ids never change.** No new fields.
- **Muscles:** ids from `muscleTree`; at least one primary; no id twice. A pattern listed in `PATTERN_PRIMARY` needs a
  primary under one of its listed muscles.
- **pattern / equipmentList / logAs / difficulty:** values from the lists.
  - `logAs` `weight-reps`/`weight-time` needs at least one item from `LOAD_EQUIPMENT`.
  - `bodyweight-reps`/`time` must have none of them.
  - `cardio` pattern ⇔ `cardio` logAs.
- **unilateral: true** means one side at a time, each side its own set. An exercise that alternates sides within a set
  is `false`. When true, the steps must contain one of "each side", "each arm", "each leg", "each hand", "other side"
  or "switch sides".
- **family:** `fam-` + kebab-case. A family with one member must be named `fam-<the id in kebab-case>` (e.g. `Plank`
  alone → `fam-plank`). Reuse an existing family name when joining one.
- **Names and aliases:** a shown name or alias may not equal (case/punctuation-insensitively) any other entry's shown
  name or alias (see `all-shown-names.txt`). No alias may be exactly "press", "row" or "machine". An alias may not
  repeat the entry's own shown name.
- **Text rules (exact):**
  - `description`: one sentence, starts with a capital, ends with a period, max 120 characters.
  - `formCues`: 2–3 lines, max 70 characters each, capital start, NO final period.
  - `steps`: 3–6 lines, max 140 characters each, capital start, each ENDS with a period, not numbered.
  - `mistakes`: 1–3 lines, max 90 characters each, capital start, NO final period.
  - **No digits** anywhere, except an angle like "45 degrees". No counted amounts ("two seconds", "three reps",
    "a few breaths"), no sets×reps, and no kg/lb/%/RPE.
  - No hype words: great, best, ultimate, amazing, excellent, perfect, effective.
  - No medical words: injury/injure…, pain, prevent…, rehab…, heal…, therap….
  - Breathing cues (inhale/exhale/breathe in/out) only inside `steps`.
  - No boilerplate: "starting position", "recommended amount", "prescribed amount", "this portion of the movement",
    "as you perform this", or lines starting "Tip:", "Caution:" or "Variation:".
  - No line may repeat another line of the same entry.

## Output

Write ONE file, `changes-N.json` (N = your entries file number), in this folder. It holds a JSON array of changes:
```
{ "id": "...", "field": "<field>", "before": <current value>, "after": <new value>, "reason": "<one line>" }
```
- `field` is one of: `displayName` (after = the new shown name, or null to remove the display name), `aliases` (after =
  the FULL new list), `description`, `formCues`, `steps`, `mistakes` (after = the full new list), `primary`,
  `secondary` (full lists of muscle ids), `pattern`, `equipmentList`, `logAs`, `unilateral`, `difficulty`, `staple`,
  `family`.
- One object per changed field. The reason is short and concrete.
- Validate each change against the hard rules before writing it.

Finish with a short summary: how many entries you changed, by field, and anything you noticed across the whole library
(inconsistent naming conventions, families that should be merged or split) that is outside your file or needs a human
call.
