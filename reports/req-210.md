**1. Arrow (DEC-117 §1).** `src/beat-last-time.js`:
- `improvementPct(thisSets, lastSets, type)` — pure, takes the two sides' work sets and the type (the spec's
  `(thisItem, lastItem)`; sets + type is what the axis needs). It reuses `compareExercise`, so it is non-null exactly when
  DEC-050 calls a win. Axis: timed → best duration; weighted → top kg, or reps at the same top kg; else best reps.
  `(this − last) / last`, `Math.round`.
