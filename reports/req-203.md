## Technical

**What changed**
1. **Day screen from Home is a date** (`views/Schedule.jsx` ScheduleDay, new pure `views/schedule-day.js`).
   - `homeDayDate` gives the date only when `from` is `/`: the weekday of now's Mon–Sun week, and only if that date's loop week is the path's week.
   - Title "Wednesday, Oct 7" (`longWeekdayDate`).
   - Done rows come first: "{name} · Done ✓ ›" for each workout finished that date (`doneOnDay` = `completedOnDayKey`, by finish date like `weekRows`). They link to `/history/:id` through `childLink`, so Back returns to the day screen.
   - `startNowShown`: past date → no; today → no once `coveringWorkout(…, date, slot.id)` finds a workout; future → yes.
2. **Home done line** (`Today.jsx` TodayRoutine): "Done ✓ — see your sets ›" → `/history/:id?from=/`.
3. **Loop line and Remove confirm**: the sub line is "Every Wednesday" or "Every Saturday in week 2 of 2", and the title no longer carries " · week N of M". Remove asks "Take {name} off {Weekday}s[ in week N]? Your history is kept."
4. **Backup & data** (`Settings.jsx`): the line above, "Back up now", "Backup saved — {filename}" (uses `exportBackup`'s returned name), "Restore from a backup". The Developer group is unchanged below.
5. **By exercise** (`history/list.jsx`, `topSetText` in `history/helpers.js`): "{date} · 25 kg × 10". The top set is the heaviest working set: no warm-up, not skipped, kg > 0, and a kg tie goes to the most reps. A row with no kg keeps "N sets".
6. **Search ×** (`ui/index.jsx` SearchField): × focuses the input before clearing.
7. **Pill** (`workout-log.js` workoutPillState): an item with no logged (non-skipped) set gets `setText ''`, so the label is the clock or GO alone. `WorkoutPill` drops the separator when there is no tail.

No schema change, and no stored-data write beyond the normal UI paths.
