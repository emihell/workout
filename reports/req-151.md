hit on a listed non-staple's own name key (shown name or free-db name) is pulled into the first tier. It goes after any
staple exact hits, so a staple that matches exactly still wins.
- **One refinement of the spec: it pulls only when there is at least one staple hit.** With no staple hit the rest
  already shows directly, and pulling would push the others behind "Show more". Without the refinement, "floor press"
  showed Floor Press and hid Dumbbell Floor Press; with it, that query is unchanged.
- Hidden entries are never pulled, even when the user owns one. So "air bike" with an owned Air Bike keeps Fan Bike
